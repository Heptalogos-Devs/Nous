# Native VCP sources

The numerical kernels are copied and adapted from lioensky/VCPToolBox at
`d5c019ebbac1f317c2938d655d2ce916e41b9164`, under the retained CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
license in LICENSE. Exact source hashes are recorded in upstream-sources.json.

geometry retains AGS and local/global EPA construction. artifact retains ordered
fact mass, calibrated transport and provenance. sensing/projection retain
predecessor propagation, dynamic existing-edge routing and bounded basin growth.
pipeline adopts conservative-topology-v2.2 residual projection, lexical focus,
conservative seed admission, fusion and both fields. river adopts current
candidate-self topology, absolute anchor activation, independent quality/ranking
scores and the strictly admitted maximum-route RRF correction. thinking consumes
that same complete predecessor observation over explicit named candidate stages.

Database reads, Node/N-API runtime, global observation caches and filesystem
business state are replaced by immutable numerical assets. Native does not read
Nous business databases or CAS storage. The owning Engine supplies one bounded
Rayon pool and cooperative cancellation. Geometry and derived graph/index state
are persisted for reopening without re-embedding or rebuilding numerical assets.

Nous extensions distinguish ordered source sequence, candidate curve document
and independent evidence root. Source occurrences retain repetition; unordered
memberships contribute equal-direction mass without invented order. Derived
candidates exclude every own root from independent support. Typed positive
relations contribute deduplicated source fact mass before transport calibration;
negative relations remain a separate readable counterevidence channel. Wormhole
witness admission reads frozen independent-root facts instead of SQL files.

Concept aliases participate in lexical grounding. Exact core identity is a
Concept row, avoiding ambiguous same-label promotion. Context role aggregation
and adjacent-vector segmentation adapt the arithmetic in ContextVectorManager.js;
source input cleanup, fuzzy reuse and global sessions are excluded. Semantic
near-duplicate folding adapts ResultDeduplicator.js norm reuse and preserves the
final ranking order, references and independent source identities.

The named dtsc module is extracted from Nous-Wave
`412b062082afb75ccfae4c3f69fc606d3e3cc862` reference/dtsc and its curve DTO/cosine
closure, under Apache-2.0. Other retired baseline algorithms and their runtime
support are not part of the current workspace. Distributed licenses are bundled
in src/nous_native/licenses.
